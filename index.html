Tasks, habits, focus & goals — in one calm, private planner.

A to-do list that understands "pay rent tomorrow at 9am", a habit tracker that keeps your streaks honest, a Pomodoro focus timer, and a calendar that stays in sync with Apple Calendar. No account. No ads. Nothing leaves your iPhone.

Free · iPhone · iOS 17 or later · Data Not Collected

Everything in one place

Plan the day, keep the habit, finish the work.

Most planners make you juggle three apps. Goaldyn puts tasks, habits, focus time, and goals on one screen, so the plan and the follow-through live together.

✅

Tasks that stay out of your way

Type or dictate "call the dentist tomorrow at 2pm" and it's scheduled. Lists, tags, priorities, subtasks, and due dates color-coded by urgency — overdue, today, tomorrow, later.

🔁

Habits that stick

Daily, weekly, or specific-day schedules. Streaks and consistency over the last 7, 30, or 90 days, and one-tap check-ins from the app or a widget.

⏱️

Deep focus

A Pomodoro-style timer with short and long breaks, a live countdown in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, and focus time that adds up per task or habit.

📅

Calendar, both ways

Day, week, and month views. Tap an empty slot to create a task right there. Two-way sync with Apple Calendar — move something in either app and the other follows.

🔲

The Eisenhower matrix

Sort everything by urgent and important, and see at a glance what to do first, schedule, delegate, or drop.

📈

Progress you can feel

Completion, habit consistency, and goal momentum at a glance. Track goals with targets and units — books read, miles run, anything.

A look inside

Calm by design.

Private by design

Your plans are nobody's business.

Goaldyn's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected" — and that's the whole architecture, not a setting.

  • No account and no sign-in — the app works the moment it's installed.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
  • Everything is stored on your device. Calendar and voice features use Apple's own on-device frameworks with your permission.
  • Delete the app and its data goes with it. Read the full policy.

Free on the App Store

For iPhone, iOS 17 or later. Home Screen widgets and Siri Shortcuts included.
