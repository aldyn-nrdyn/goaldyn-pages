Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Goaldyn is a personal planner for tasks, habits, goals, and focus time. It was designed to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information.

The short version

Goaldyn does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. The app has no user accounts, does no tracking, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. Everything you create stays on your device, unless you choose to add events to a calendar your iPhone syncs with an account (see Calendar below).

What stays on your device

Your tasks, events, lists, habits, goals, focus sessions, and settings are stored only in a local database on your iPhone. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. The only copies Goaldyn makes are the events it adds to your calendar if you turn on calendar sync (see Calendar below).

Calendar (optional)

Goaldyn asks for calendar access only when you tap Allow on the Calendar tab or turn on a calendar option in Settings > Calendar. The app works fully without it.

Showing your calendars. With access allowed and "Show my other calendars" on, Goaldyn reads the events in the calendars already on your iPhone (title, time, location, link, notes, and the calendar and account they belong to) and shows them next to your plans, on the Calendar tab and in the Schedule on the Today tab. This is read-only: Goaldyn does not change these events. They are read on your device when they are shown and are not saved in Goaldyn unless you tap "Add to Goaldyn" on an event. You choose which calendars appear, or turn this off, in Settings > Calendar.

Sync with your calendar. If you turn this on, Goaldyn adds your Goaldyn events, and your tasks with a time unless you turn that part off, to the calendar you pick (your iPhone's default calendar until you choose another, or a new "Goaldyn" calendar if you ask for one). It also reads back the edits you make to them in the Calendar app (for example moving or renaming one) so both stay in step. Goaldyn changes or deletes only the events it added or the events you added to Goaldyn; it leaves the rest of your calendar alone.

All of this happens on your device through Apple's EventKit framework. Goaldyn never signs in to a calendar account, and your calendar data never leaves your device through Goaldyn: it is never sent to us or to anyone else. If the calendar you pick belongs to an account your iPhone already syncs (for example iCloud), iOS keeps the events in it in sync with that account, as it does for any calendar event.

Voice task entry (optional)

If you use the microphone button to dictate a task and grant microphone and speech-recognition permission, Goaldyn uses Apple's speech recognition to turn what you say into text. On iPhones that support it, recognition happens on the device. Where on-device recognition is unavailable, audio may be processed by Apple's speech service under Apple's privacy policy. Goaldyn itself never records, stores, or transmits your audio; only the resulting text appears in your task.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Goaldyn schedules local reminders on your device. These are handled entirely by iOS and are not transmitted anywhere.

No data collected

Consistent with the above, Goaldyn's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected." Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for you to request, correct, or delete on our side — deleting the app removes its local data from your device. Events Goaldyn added to your calendar stay in that calendar; to remove them, use Settings > Calendar > Remove Goaldyn events from calendar before deleting the app.

Children

Goaldyn does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its "last updated" date.
