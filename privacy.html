Privacy Policy — Goaldyn

Last updated: July 2026

Goaldyn is a personal planner for tasks, habits, goals, and focus time. We designed it to be private by default. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information.

The short version

Goaldyn does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of your data. The app has no user accounts, does no tracking, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or third‑party advertising or tracking SDKs. Everything you create stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Your tasks, lists, habits, goals, focus sessions, and settings are stored only in a local database on your iPhone or iPad. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

Calendar (optional)

If you turn on calendar export and grant permission, Goaldyn adds your dated tasks and events to your own device calendar using Apple's EventKit. That data goes only to the calendar you already use on your device; it is not sent to Goaldyn.

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, Goaldyn schedules local reminders on your device. These are handled entirely by iOS and are not transmitted anywhere.

No data collected

Consistent with the above, Goaldyn's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected." Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for you to request, correct, or delete on our side — deleting the app removes its local data from your device.

Children

Goaldyn does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its "last updated" date.
