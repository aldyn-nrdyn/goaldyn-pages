Goaldyn Support

Goaldyn is a private planner for tasks, habits, goals, and deep-focus time — all on your iPhone.

Get help

Email info@nrdyn.com and we'll get back to you. Please include your iOS version and a short description of what you were doing.

Common questions

Where is my data? Everything lives on your iPhone. Goaldyn has no account and sends nothing anywhere itself, so your tasks and habits stay private to your device. The one exception is your choice: if you turn on calendar sync, your events are added to the calendar you pick, and if that calendar belongs to an account your iPhone syncs, iOS syncs them with it.

How do I add a task quickly? Type into the "Add a task" field on the Tasks tab — phrases like "tomorrow at 9am" or "today at 5pm" set the date and time automatically. Tap the microphone to dictate instead.

What do the colors on due dates mean? Red is overdue, orange is due today, gold is due tomorrow, and grey is later — so the next thing that needs you is always obvious.

How does calendar sync work? In Settings > Calendar, turn on "Sync with your calendar", allow calendar access, and pick a calendar under "Add events to". Your Goaldyn events, and tasks with a time if you leave "Include tasks with a time" on, appear in that calendar; move or edit one in the Calendar app and Goaldyn follows. If you delete a synced event in the Calendar app, Goaldyn moves that event to its Trash, where you can restore it. If you delete a synced task there, the task just loses its date; the task itself is never deleted. To take Goaldyn's events back out of your calendar, use "Remove Goaldyn events from calendar" in Settings > Calendar; everything in Goaldyn stays.

Can I see my other calendars in Goaldyn? Yes. Tap Allow on the Calendar tab. Events from the calendars already on your iPhone then appear next to your plans, on the Calendar tab and in the Schedule on the Today tab, each in its calendar's color. Goaldyn only reads them: tap one for its details and, where the event allows it, "Add to Goaldyn". Choose which calendars show, or turn this off, in Settings > Calendar.

How do habits and streaks work? Check a habit on each day it's scheduled; Goaldyn keeps a per-day log and shows your streak and consistency. You can choose the window the Progress tab uses — last 7, 30, or 90 days, or all time — in Settings.

What is the focus timer? A Pomodoro-style timer with short and long breaks. It shows in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, and you can link a session to a task or habit so your focus time adds up in the statistics.

Can I create an event or a task from the calendar? Yes — tap an empty time slot in the day, 3-day, or week view, or use the + button. A new event opens with its start and end filled in; use the Event | Task switch at the top if you want a task instead.

How do I remove the app and its data? Delete Goaldyn from your iPhone like any app; that removes everything Goaldyn stored. Events Goaldyn added to your calendar stay in that calendar. To remove them, use Settings > Calendar > Remove Goaldyn events from calendar before you delete the app.
