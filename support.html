Common questions

Where is my data? Everything lives on your device. Goaldyn has no account and sends nothing to the cloud, so your tasks and habits stay private to your iPhone or iPad.

How do I add tasks to my calendar? Enable calendar export in Settings and grant permission; dated tasks and events are then added to your device calendar.

How do habits and streaks work? Check a habit in each day it's scheduled; Goaldyn keeps a per‑day log and shows your streak, month heatmap, and consistency.

What is the focus timer? A Pomodoro‑style timer with breaks, Live Activities, and focus statistics to help you concentrate.
