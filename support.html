Goaldyn Support

Goaldyn is a private planner for tasks, habits, goals, and deep-focus time — all on your iPhone.

Get help

Email info@nrdyn.com and we'll get back to you. Please include your iOS version and a short description of what you were doing.

Common questions

Where is my data? Everything lives on your iPhone. Goaldyn has no account and sends nothing to the cloud, so your tasks and habits stay private to your device.

How do I add a task quickly? Type into the "Add a task" field on the Tasks tab — phrases like "tomorrow at 9am" or "today at 5pm" set the date and time automatically. Tap the microphone to dictate instead.

What do the colors on due dates mean? Red is overdue, orange is due today, gold is due tomorrow, and grey is later — so the next thing that needs you is always obvious.

How does Apple Calendar sync work? Turn it on in Settings and grant calendar permission. Dated tasks appear in your calendar, and if you move or rename one of those events in the Calendar app, Goaldyn updates the task to match. Deleting the event just removes the task's date; it never deletes the task.

How do habits and streaks work? Check a habit on each day it's scheduled; Goaldyn keeps a per-day log and shows your streak and consistency. You can choose the window the Progress tab uses — last 7, 30, or 90 days, or all time — in Settings.

What is the focus timer? A Pomodoro-style timer with short and long breaks. It shows in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen, and you can link a session to a task or habit so your focus time adds up in the statistics.

Can I create a task from the calendar? Yes — tap an empty time slot in the day or week view, or use the + button, and the task is created at that time.

How do I remove the app and its data? Delete Goaldyn from your iPhone like any app; because nothing is stored anywhere else, that removes everything.
